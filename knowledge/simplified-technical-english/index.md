# Simplified Technical English

A compounding knowledge base documenting STE100-inspired Simplified Technical
English practices for technical prose output. Each concept captures a specific
writing discipline that makes technical documentation unambiguous, translatable,
and easy to read for non-native speakers and for AI agents that must execute
steps precisely.

This bundle is the canonical, publicly-reachable home for the STE100-inspired
guidelines. The build-time include
`includes/ste100-simplified-technical-english.md.tmpl` carries the gist (inlined
into skills and other bundles at build time); the concept pages here carry the
full depth.

## Concepts

* [Overview](overview.md) - Synthesis of the STE100-inspired practice set and how the pieces fit together
* [Simplified Technical English](simplified-technical-english.md) - The core guidelines: active voice, short sentences, one-word-one-meaning, imperative mood, approved modifiers, acronym definitions, and the quick self-check
* [Detailed Guide](detailed-guide.md) - Full writing rules (10 rules), approved-words guidance, before/after examples, and the 10-step self-check protocol
