# Product Case Studies

Case studies are how product managers learn from decisions they did not have
to make themselves. Each study in this collection examines a real company's
product trajectory — what they built, why, and what happened as a result.

The value is in the specifics: the constraints, the trade-offs, and the
sequence of decisions. Read these to build a mental library of patterns you
can recognize when they appear in your own work.
