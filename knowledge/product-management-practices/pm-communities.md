# PM Communities

Product management can be an isolating role — you are often the only PM in a
room of engineers, designers, and business stakeholders. Communities provide
the peer network that makes the role sustainable: people who understand the
ambiguity, the trade-offs, and the wins.

This directory covers groups, meetups by region, and conferences. Find a
local meetup to build a peer network, and attend a conference to see how the
discipline is evolving at scale.
