# PM Career Prep

This concept covers PM-specific career preparation: hiring from the employer's
side, resume guidance, interview preparation, and company-type deep dives. For
the general hiring pipeline — ATS optimization, behavioral interview
frameworks, salary negotiation, recruiter communication — see the
[Career Advancement Practices](https://github.com/levonk/skills-releases/blob/main/knowledge/career-advancement-practices/overview.md)
bundle. This page covers what is specific to product management.

## Hiring a Product Manager

Two sources address the hiring side:

- Mark Stephan's "Inspire Me with your Job Description" covers writing job
  descriptions that attract inspired PMs.[^stephan-inspire-job-desc]
- Todd Jackson (VP of Product and Design at Dropbox) covers how to find, vet,
  and close the best PMs.[^jackson-find-vet-close]

## Resume Preparation

PM-specific resume resources include coaching services, sample resumes, and
structured guides. Product Alliance offers a lifetime-access course on
crafting a PM resume.[^product-alliance-resume] APMList offers $25 resume
reviews by a PM at Yahoo.[^apmlist-reviews] Quora hosts community advice on
writing PM resumes.[^quora-pm-resume]

For the general resume architecture and writing practices (metrics,
ownership verbs, keyword engineering, visual scanning patterns), see
[Career Advancement Practices — Resume System Architecture](https://github.com/levonk/skills-releases/blob/main/knowledge/career-advancement-practices/resume-architecture-and-lineage.md).

## Where to Apply

Two PM-specific job boards:

- [ProductHired.com](https://www.producthired.com/) — PM job search[^producthired-jobs]
- [PathtoProduct.com](https://pathtoproduct.com/) — over 1,000 product jobs at
  startups and tech companies[^pathtoproduct]

For general job board strategy (LinkedIn, Indeed, specialized boards,
easy-apply trap, referral-first principle), see
[Career Advancement Practices — Job Board Strategy](https://github.com/levonk/skills-releases/blob/main/knowledge/career-advancement-practices/job-board-strategy.md).

## Interview Preparation

PM interviews differ from general interviews — they include product sense
questions, estimation questions, and execution questions. Key resources:

- Exponent's guide to preparing in 15-20 hours[^exponent-prep]
- Product Alliance's "Hacking the PM Interview" — 16 hours of video lessons
  and 62 pages of strategy guides[^product-alliance-hacking]
- "Cracking the PM Interview" by Jackie Bavaro — the canonical
  book[^cracking-pm-interview]
- The Ultimate Guide to PM Interview Questions[^ultimate-pm-questions]
- PM Job Interview Exercises — free repository of questions and
  answers[^pm-interview-exercises]
- Exponent — online courses, free interview questions database, and Slack
  community for mock interviews[^exponent-community]

For general interview frameworks (STAR/PAR/CAR, behavioral interviews,
standard questions, case interviews, presentation interviews), see
[Career Advancement Practices — Interview Strategy](https://github.com/levonk/skills-releases/blob/main/knowledge/career-advancement-practices/interview-strategy.md).

## Company Types

PM culture varies significantly by company. Product Alliance's Deep Dives
course covers the top 22 companies for PM (FAANG, LinkedIn, Uber, Airbnb,
Pinterest, Twitter, etc.) with exclusive interviews and 200+ pages of
strategy guides.[^product-alliance-deep-dives] The Google APM Interview Guide
explains each stage of the Google APM interview with practice
questions.[^google-apm-guide]
