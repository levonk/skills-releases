# Sample Product Documentation

Reading real product documents is one of the fastest ways to internalize the
shape of product work. Specs, PRDs, usability tests, and go-to-market plans
each have a structure that practitioners refine over years. Studying examples
from real companies — warts and all — teaches you what a good document
contains and what it deliberately leaves out.

This collection gathers sample documents across three categories: product
specs, usability tests, and go-to-market plans. Use them as templates and
inspiration, not as rigid formulas.
