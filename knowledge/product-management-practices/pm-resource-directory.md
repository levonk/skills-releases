# PM Resource Directory

A working product manager needs a toolkit that spans the full product
lifecycle — from discovery and design through development, launch, and
post-launch analytics. This directory catalogs the tools that practitioners
reach for most often, grouped by function.

The list is not exhaustive. It is a starting point: enough coverage to get a
new PM productive quickly, with enough breadth to serve as a reference for
experienced PMs evaluating alternatives in a given category.

## Resource Repositories

* [Product Management Toolkit](https://www.productmanagementkit.com/) — Curated directory of tools and resources for PMs[^product-management-kit]
* [Software Product Management Stack](https://github.com/ProductHired/open-product-management) — A curated list of PM articles and resources[^open-product-management]
* [End-End Product Management Lifecycle](https://www.productmanagementkit.com/) — A list of articles from what is it to becoming a great PM[^product-management-kit]
* [Harvard Business School PM 101/102](https://www.hbs.edu/) — Online resource repository from HBS PM 101 & 102[^hbs-pm]
* [Product Management Processes](https://www.productmanagementprocesses.com/) — A knowledge base of PM processes, tools and templates[^product-management-processes]
* [ProductNotion](https://productnotion.com/) — Ready-made PM templates for Notion.so[^productnotion]

## Design

* [Figma](https://www.figma.com/) — Collaborative interface design tool[^figma]
* [Adobe XD](https://www.adobe.com/products/xd.html) — End-to-end free solution for designing & prototyping[^adobe-xd]
* [Facebook Design Resources](https://design.facebook.com/) — Free resources by designers at Facebook[^facebook-design]
* [IconStore](https://iconstore.co/) — Free icon packs by first-class designers[^iconstore]
* [UI Movement](https://uimovement.com/) — Best UI design inspiration, daily[^ui-movement]
* [Coverr](https://coverr.co/) — Beautiful, free videos for homepage backgrounds[^coverr]

## Development

* [Craft](https://www.craft.io/) — Collaborative Place for Product Managers, Agile Product & Project Management Software[^craft]
* [Android Studio](https://developer.android.com/studio) — Official IDE for Android application development[^android-studio]
* [Xcode](https://developer.apple.com/xcode/) — iOS Development[^xcode]
* [The Step-By-Step PM Guide to Building Machine Learning Based Products](https://www.mindtheproduct.com/) — 6 step guide[^mind-the-product-ml]

## Mockups and Marketing

* [moqups](https://moqups.com/) — Streamlined web app for wireframes, mockups, diagrams and prototypes[^moqups]
* [Typeform](https://www.typeform.com/) — Generate surveys in seconds[^typeform]
* [Balsamiq](https://balsamiq.com/) — Rapid wireframing tool[^balsamiq]
* [Flinto for Mac](https://www.flinto.com/) — Prototyping tool[^flinto]
* [LaunchKit](https://github.com/launchkit/) — Web-based tools for mobile app developers, open source[^launchkit]

## Team Management

* [Airtable](https://airtable.com/) — Spreadsheet, meet database[^airtable]
* [Trello](https://trello.com/) — Organize anything with anyone[^trello]
* [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/) — Create and share work online[^google-drive]
* [Slack](https://slack.com/) — Team communication[^slack]
* [Evernote](https://evernote.com/) — Note taking[^evernote]
* [Quip](https://quip.com/) — Productivity suite for mobile[^quip]
* [Smartsheet](https://www.smartsheet.com/) — Microsoft Project alternative[^smartsheet]
* [Zenkit](https://zenkit.com/) — Better Trello, with Mindmaps[^zenkit]

## Analytics

* [Matomo](https://matomo.org/) — All-in-one free web analytics platform[^matomo]
* [KISSmetrics](https://kissmetrics.com/) — Understand what people do on your website[^kissmetrics]
* [Google Analytics 360 Suite](https://marketingplatform.google.com/) — Enterprise analytics for marketing[^google-analytics-360]
* [Heap Analytics](https://heap.io/) — Automatically capture every user interaction[^heap-analytics]
* [Snowplow Analytics](https://snowplow.io/) — Open source web, mobile and event analytics[^snowplow-analytics]
* [Mixpanel](https://mixpanel.com/) — Monitor key metrics[^mixpanel]
