# PM Media Resources

Staying current in product management requires a steady intake of practitioner
perspectives. Newsletters deliver curated signal, podcasts expose reasoning in
conversation, courses build structured foundations, and blogs capture
hard-won lessons from the trenches.

This directory groups media by format. Pick one or two from each category to
build a lightweight learning habit rather than subscribing to everything at
once.

## Newsletters

* [Weekly Thoughts of Tim Herbig](https://www.timherbig.com/) — A weekly digest of personal advice for product people[^tim-herbig]
* [Austin Voice of Product](https://www.austinvoiceofproduct.com/) — Product newsletter for the Austin product community[^austin-voice-of-product]

## Podcasts

* [Design Details Podcast](https://designdetails.fm/) — A show about the people who design our favorite products[^design-details]
* [Exponent — Get Off My Lawn!](https://exponent.fm/) — How Snapchat has built its product effectively[^exponent]
* [Products That Count](https://productsthatcount.com/) — By Sophie-Charlotte Moatti[^products-that-count]
* [This is Product Management](https://thisisproductmanagement.com/) — By AlphaUX[^this-is-product-management]
* [Agile Uprising Podcast](https://agileuprising.com/) — Covers all topics associated with Agile[^agile-uprising]
* [mindtheproduct.com Podcasts](https://www.mindtheproduct.com/) — Mind the Product podcast feed[^mind-the-product-podcasts]
* [Masters of Scale with Reid Hoffman](https://mastersofscale.com/) — How do companies grow from zero to a gazillion?[^masters-of-scale]

## Online Courses

* [Product Alliance](https://productalliance.com/) — All the tools you need to land your dream PM job[^product-alliance]
* [Product School](https://productschool.com/) — Get a Product Manager Job[^product-school]
* [Product Management — General Assembly](https://generalassemb.ly/) — 10-week part-time course[^general-assembly]
* [Coursera — Software Product Management](https://www.coursera.org/) — Create Better Software using Agile Practices[^coursera-spm]
* [Pragmatic Marketing Courses](https://pragmaticinstitute.com/) — Product Management and Marketing Training[^pragmatic-institute]
* [Digital Product Management — Modern Fundamentals — UVA Darden](https://www.coursera.org/) — University of Virginia Darden School[^coursera-darden]
* [AI Product Manager](https://www.linkedin.com/learning/) — Learn to develop AI products[^linkedin-learning-ai-pm]

## Online Blogs

* [The Cranky Product Manager](https://crankyproductmanager.com/) — A blog on PM, product marketing, and the ugly side of software product development[^cranky-product-manager]
* [Street Smart Product Manager](https://streetsmartproductmanager.com/) — Thriving in the urban jungle of product[^street-smart-product-manager]
* [Product Talk](https://www.producttalk.org/) — Make better product decisions[^product-talk]
* [Silicon Valley Product Group](https://www.svpg.com/) — Shares lessons learned and knowledge of PM[^svpg]
* [The Clever PM](https://thecleverpm.com/) — Tips, tricks, and hacks for being a better PM[^the-clever-pm]
* [Mind the Product](https://www.mindtheproduct.com/) — Product Management Blog and events[^mind-the-product-blog]
* [Good Product Manager](https://goodproductmanager.com/) — How To Be A Good Product Manager[^good-product-manager]
* [15 Things You Should Know About Product Managers](https://cutle.fish/) — John Cutler[^cutle-fish]
* [The Black Box of Product Management](https://blackboxofpm.com/) — Shining light on the PM discipline[^blackbox-of-pm]
