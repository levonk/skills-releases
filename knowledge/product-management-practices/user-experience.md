# User Experience

The product manager's relationship with user experience is collaborative but
distinct. The PM owns the problem and the outcome; UX owns the interaction
design and the user's journey through the product. The curated sources below
cover two practical frameworks: the Lean UX Canvas for structuring UX
hypotheses, and the concept of user experience debt — the accumulated cost of
UX compromises made under shipping pressure.

## Lean UX and Hypothesis-Driven Design

The Lean UX Canvas is a tool for structuring assumptions, hypotheses, and
experiments in a way that aligns the cross-functional team around learning
rather than deliverables.[^stephan-lean-ux-canvas] It complements customer
development by giving the team a shared artifact for tracking what they
believe and what they have validated.

## User Experience Debt

Vijay Sundaram's concept of user experience debt parallels technical debt:
every UX shortcut compounds over time, and eventually the interest payments
make the product harder to use.[^sundaram-ux-debt] The PM's job is to name
this debt, make it visible, and budget time to pay it down.
