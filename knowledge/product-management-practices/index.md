# Product Management Practices

A compounding knowledge base documenting product management as a discipline —
from the definition of the role through team organization, leadership, and
career entry. Each concept organizes curated practitioner sources (articles,
essays, talks) by topic, with framing that connects the sources into a
coherent practice area.

The bundle is structured around four cores drawn from the Open Product
Management catalog (ProductHired/open-product-management):

1. **Product Management** — the discipline itself (what it is, how to do it,
   how to lead)
2. **Resources** — curated tools, books, media, and courses for PMs
3. **Real Life Product Management** — practitioner interviews, company case
   studies, sample documentation, and communities
4. **Get the Job** — PM-specific career preparation, cross-linked to the
   [Career Advancement Practices](https://github.com/levonk/skills-releases/blob/main/knowledge/career-advancement-practices/index.md)
   bundle for the general hiring pipeline
