# PM Practitioner Interviews

There is no single path into product management. Practitioners arrive from
engineering, design, consulting, and even completely unrelated fields. The
interviews in this collection — conducted by Jason Shah as part of his "How
to Get into Product Management" series — capture the real career paths of
working PMs and the specific moves that got them in the door.

Read these for the texture of real transitions, not for a formula. The
common thread is that each person found a way to do product work before
having the title.

## Interviews

* **Janna Bastow** — Founder of ProdPad, previously a PM/consultant. On building a product company from the PM seat.
* **Eva Andreasson** — Senior PM at Cloudera. On moving into product from a technical background.
* **Teresa Torres** — VP Products at AfterCollege, product consultant. On continuous discovery and the PM craft.
* **Wil Keenan** — Senior PM at Change.org. On product for social impact.
* **Chris Lee** — PM at Dropbox. On the transition from engineering to product.
* **Raj Ramachandran** — Director of Product at Kidaptive, former PM at Zynga. On growing from IC PM to product leader.
* **Pascal Carole** — PM at Microsoft/Yammer, former engineer at Microsoft. On making the internal switch from engineering to PM.
* **Jackie Bavaro** — PM at Asana. On the craft of product management and co-authoring Cracking the PM Interview.
* **Jason Evanish** — PM at KISSMetrics. On PM in a data-driven startup environment.
* **Ryan Hoover** — Director of Product, PlayHaven. On building Product Hunt and the founder-PM overlap.
* **Marty Cagan and others** — Why they love the role, what they look for when hiring graduates. On what distinguishes great PMs from good ones.
