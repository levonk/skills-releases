# Pre-Booking Verification Workflow

The one exercise that converts every reading discipline in this
bundle into a deliverable. Run it before paying a deposit — it takes
under 30 minutes and works whether the itinerary arrived as a glossy
PDF, a portal link, or a string of chat messages.

## Step 1 — Rebuild the Day-by-Day

Open a blank document and rebuild the itinerary as a two-column
table: **"What it says"** on the left, **"What this actually means /
what I need to budget or confirm"** on the right. Fill in:

- every [missing meal](meal-codes-and-gap-budgeting.md)
- every ["panoramic" and "at leisure"](itinerary-verb-decoding.md)
- every [long drive](transit-time-verification.md) and
  [check-in gap](check-in-out-timing-gaps.md)
- every [conditional weather activity](conditional-activities-and-force-majeure.md)
- every [excluded fee](inclusions-exclusions-analysis.md)

This routinely surfaces $200–500 in unbudgeted costs per trip.

## Step 2 — Send the Right Column Back as Numbered Questions

Email questions in a **numbered list** and ask for written
confirmation: *"Please confirm in writing: (1) temple admission
included? (2) transfer driver's contact? (3) hotel names for all
nights?"* A written reply becomes part of your contract record.

High-value questions by section:

- **Hotels** — the named property for each night (not "or similar");
  see [Hotel Substitution](hotel-substitution-and-guide-model.md)
- **Transfers** — does the included transfer cover your flight, or
  only the group's main flight?
- **Conditionals** — "If [the weather-dependent activity] doesn't
  run, what specifically happens?"
- **Optionals** — which optional excursions need advance booking, and
  what are the deadlines? See
  [Optional Excursions](optional-excursions-tradeoff.md)
- **Flights** — e-ticket numbers if flights are included (a
  13-character number means issued, not held); see
  [Pricing Codes](pricing-codes-and-solo-supplements.md)
- **Contact** — the in-destination operator's name, local phone, and
  WhatsApp — worth more than the head-office email when a transfer
  doesn't show
- **Solo travelers** — "Do you have any single-supplement-waived
  departures?"

## Step 3 — Calendar the Cancellation Schedule

The cancellation and amendment terms live at the very back of the
document. Find the date the deposit becomes non-refundable and the
penalty schedule (often 25% / 50% / 100% as the trip nears). Note
them in your calendar — every verification question above has a hard
deadline attached, and it's that date.
