# Live Itinerary Version Control

A growing share of itineraries never arrive as a PDF — platforms and
operator back-ends deliver them through apps and web portals, and
many small operators run the whole trip through a WhatsApp group,
drip-feeding tomorrow's pickup time the night before. Convenient —
but two new reading risks appear.

## The Two Risks

1. **A live itinerary can change silently.** The "Day 4" you booked
   may quietly update in the app with no email trail. **Screenshot
   the version you paid for** — it's your reference point in any
   dispute.
2. **The legally binding terms live somewhere else.** A friendly
   WhatsApp message is not your contract. The inclusions, exclusions,
   and cancellation schedule are in the booking confirmation or the
   platform's terms of service — not the chat.

## Practice

- **Ask the operator to confirm key details in a savable format** —
  hotel names, what's included, refund terms — even when the trip is
  being coordinated over chat. Written replies become part of your
  contract record (same mechanism as the
  [Pre-Booking Verification Workflow](pre-booking-verification-workflow.md)).
- **Save offline copies of everything versioned** — the itinerary as
  booked, the confirmation, the cancellation schedule. App versions
  update; your copies don't.
- **Re-check the live version before departure.** Compare the current
  in-app itinerary against your screenshot — silent changes are
  cheaper to dispute before you fly than after.
