# Meal Codes and Gap Budgeting

Itineraries mark included meals with letters after each day:
**B / L / D** = Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner. The absence of a letter is
information — a day that reads "(B)" only means lunch and dinner are
on your own dime.

## Practice

- **Count the missing meals, don't scan them.** Over a 10-day trip,
  tally every missing L and D and convert the count to money at
  destination prices — a dinner gap is ~$40–60 in Reykjavik and ~$6
  in Hanoi. The same itinerary costs different amounts to *complete*
  depending on where it runs.
- **Check what "included" includes.** "Dinner included" often means
  food only — drinks with included meals are routinely extra (see
  [Inclusions and Exclusions](inclusions-exclusions-analysis.md)).
- **Fold the meal gap into tour comparison.** A cheaper tour with
  more missing meals can be the more expensive product; compare
  all-in, not headline (see
  [Inclusions and Exclusions](inclusions-exclusions-analysis.md)).

## Worked scale

| Destination class | Typical self-paid dinner |
|-------------------|--------------------------|
| High-cost (Iceland, Switzerland, Scandinavia) | $40–60+ |
| Mid-cost (Southern Europe, Japan cities) | $15–30 |
| Low-cost (SE Asia, parts of Latin America) | $5–15 |
