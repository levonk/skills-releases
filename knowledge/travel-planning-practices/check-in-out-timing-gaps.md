# Check-In/Out Timing Gaps

"Day 3: Arrive Santorini, check in to hotel" hides a question: what
time? Hotel check-in worldwide is typically 2:00–3:00 PM and checkout
is around noon. A 9:00 AM arrival leaves five hours with luggage and
no room; a 9:00 PM departure after a noon checkout leaves a whole
afternoon.

## Practice

- **Compare arrival/departure times against the room window.** For
  every day that says "arrive" or "depart," check the actual flight,
  ferry, or train time against the 14:00–15:00 check-in and ~12:00
  checkout windows. Long gaps are solvable — but only if you know
  they exist.
- **Look for the "subject to availability" language.** A good
  itinerary states "early check-in subject to availability." A vague
  one leaves you guessing — ask explicitly whether early check-in or
  day-use rooms are arranged on early arrivals.
- **Ask for the fallback plan in writing.** Late checkout and luggage
  storage are the standard fixes; both are commonly available but
  rarely promised unless asked. Add them to the numbered questions in
  the
  [Pre-Booking Verification Workflow](pre-booking-verification-workflow.md).
- **Transit days amplify gaps.** On
  [long transit days](transit-time-verification.md), a hotel change
  plus a late-afternoon arrival often means the room is the only
  thing the day delivers — budget the day accordingly.
