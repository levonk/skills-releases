# Itinerary Types

The biggest reading mistake is assuming one itinerary type works like
another. The same line item — "Day 4: Chiang Mai" — means a guide
handles everything on an escorted group tour, and a hotel voucher plus
a phone number on a self-guided package. Identify the type first; it
changes how every other section reads.

## The Four Types

| Type | Who issues it | What "included" usually means | Flexibility |
|------|---------------|-------------------------------|-------------|
| Escorted group tour | Operators (Intrepid, G Adventures, Trafalgar) | Transport, lodging, listed meals, a guide | Low — fixed dates and pace |
| Private custom itinerary | A DMC (Destination Management Company) or travel agent | Whatever you negotiated, line by line | High before booking, low after |
| Self-guided / FIT package | OTAs or agents (FIT = Free Independent Traveler) | Lodging + transfers + activity vouchers; you navigate | Medium |
| Cruise shore-excursion sheet | The cruise line | A few hours on land, port-to-port | Very low |

## Reading Rules by Type

- **Escorted group**: assume logistics are handled; concentrate on
  [pace](pace-and-day-count-traps.md),
  [optional excursions](optional-excursions-tradeoff.md), and
  [excluded costs](inclusions-exclusions-analysis.md) (tips, visas,
  city taxes).
- **Private custom**: "included" is literally what the document lists
  — nothing is implied. Negotiate line items *before* booking; after
  payment the document is the contract.
- **Self-guided / FIT**: every "at leisure" and transfer line needs a
  who-drives-whom answer. Confirm vouchers cover the transfers you
  expect and get the on-the-ground contact before departure (see
  [Pre-Booking Verification](pre-booking-verification-workflow.md)).
- **Cruise excursion sheet**: note the *all-aboard* time, not the
  docking time — the ship leaves on schedule. Operator excursions
  guarantee the ship waits; independent tours that don't guarantee
  return-to-port are a real risk.
