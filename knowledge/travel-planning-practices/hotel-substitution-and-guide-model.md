# Hotel Substitution and Guide Model

Two details determine what the trip actually feels like — where you
sleep and who (if anyone) is with you — and both hide behind
comfortable-sounding phrasing.

## "4-Star Hotel or Similar"

"Or similar" is the phrase to fear. It lets the operator substitute a
property that is technically 4-star but 40 minutes from the center —
same rating, different trip.

- **Ask for the named hotels, then look them up on a map.** Location
  is the variable star rating doesn't capture; a well-run operator
  names properties per night (see
  [Well-Written Itinerary Benchmark](well-written-itinerary-benchmark.md)).
- **If names aren't available yet**, ask what neighborhoods the
  operator's typical properties sit in — a weaker answer, but it
  still converts "4-star" into something checkable.

## The Guiding Model

"With English-speaking guide" attached to *activities* rather than
the *trip* means you're alone between activities. Many itineraries
use local guides per city, not one escort throughout.

- **Full escort** — one guide travels with the group the whole trip.
- **Per-city local guides** — guiding is listed per activity; you're
  on your own for transit days, evenings, and
  [free-time blocks](itinerary-verb-decoding.md).
- **Self-guided** — no guide at all beyond orientation materials;
  normal on [FIT packages](itinerary-types.md), a surprise anywhere
  else.

Identify the model before assuming someone handles problems
mid-trip — on a per-city model, the in-destination emergency contact
matters more (see
[Pre-Booking Verification Workflow](pre-booking-verification-workflow.md)).
