# Well-Written Itinerary Benchmark

Most of this bundle is about spotting problems — but a strong
operator's document has a recognizable shape. Use this as the
positive benchmark when comparing operators or evaluating a document
before deposit.

## The Checklist

A genuinely well-written tour itinerary:

- [ ] **States named hotels**, not just "4-star or similar," with the
      city or neighborhood for each night — see
      [Hotel Substitution](hotel-substitution-and-guide-model.md)
- [ ] **Lists meal codes (B/L/D) per day** so gaps are visible at a
      glance — see [Meal Codes](meal-codes-and-gap-budgeting.md)
- [ ] **Gives realistic drive times and distances** for transfer
      days, not just town-to-town arrows — see
      [Transit Time Verification](transit-time-verification.md)
- [ ] **Spells out check-in/check-out times** and flags long luggage
      gaps — see [Timing Gaps](check-in-out-timing-gaps.md)
- [ ] **Separates inclusions and exclusions clearly**, with visas,
      taxes, tips, and drinks each addressed by name — see
      [Inclusions/Exclusions](inclusions-exclusions-analysis.md)
- [ ] **Prices optional excursions up front**, rather than leaving
      them to be sold by the guide mid-trip — see
      [Optional Excursions](optional-excursions-tradeoff.md)
- [ ] **Defines what happens if a conditional activity is cancelled**
      — re-attempt, substitute, or credit — see
      [Force Majeure](conditional-activities-and-force-majeure.md)
- [ ] **Includes a clear cancellation and amendment schedule** with
      specific dates and penalty percentages — see
      [Verification Workflow](pre-booking-verification-workflow.md)
- [ ] **Identifies the guiding model** — full escort vs per-city
      local guides vs self-guided — see
      [Guide Model](hotel-substitution-and-guide-model.md)
- [ ] **Provides a booking reference and an emergency/operator
      contact** reachable in-destination — see
      [Verification Workflow](pre-booking-verification-workflow.md)

## Reading the Score

- **Checks most boxes** → an operator who respects your time and
  money; the document is doing honest work.
- **Checks almost none** → the polish on page one is doing work the
  fine print won't back up. The missing items aren't necessarily
  deal-breakers — they are the exact list of questions to ask before
  paying, fed straight into the
  [Pre-Booking Verification Workflow](pre-booking-verification-workflow.md).
