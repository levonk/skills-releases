# Transit Time Verification

Itineraries love to compress geography. "Day 5: Drive from Queenstown
to Franz Josef" reads like a line item — it's a 5.5-hour drive
through mountain roads. A day listing three towns and a glacier can
be 80% bus.

## Practice

- **Check the two longest "drive" days on a map before accepting the
  itinerary.** Don't trust the brochure's implied schedule — measure
  the actual distance and road time yourself.
- **Apply the 5-hour rule.** If a day involves more than ~5 hours of
  transit, it *is* a transit day. Mentally cross out the sightseeing
  the brochure implies you'll squeeze in around it — the real
  deliverable that day is relocation (and it intersects with
  [check-in timing gaps](check-in-out-timing-gaps.md) on arrival).
- **Reweight the itinerary by experience hours, not days.** A trip
  heavy on long drives delivers less touring per dollar than its
  day count suggests — relevant when comparing against the
  [day-count conventions](pace-and-day-count-traps.md) of competing
  operators.
- **A well-written itinerary does this for you.** Operators who
  respect your time list realistic drive times and distances for
  transfer days — its presence is a quality signal (see
  [Well-Written Itinerary Benchmark](well-written-itinerary-benchmark.md)),
  its absence is a question to ask.
