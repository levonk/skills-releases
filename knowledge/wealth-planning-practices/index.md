# Wealth Planning Practices

A compounding knowledge base for personal wealth planning across the
whole net-worth ladder — from first emergency fund to family office.
The organizing model is a **threshold-based tier system**: what you
should do next depends on where your net worth and income sit, because
the right professionals, documents, insurance levels, and structures
change at each band.

The practices are synthesized from a curated wealth-planning reference
collection — practitioner and educator material (Christopher Nelson's
WealthOps / Micro Family Office framework, Nelson Nash–lineage
infinite-banking literature, Codie Sanchez's boring-business
acquisition canon), public tax and compliance references, and
wealth-management industry guidance. They are general US guidance —
state law, current IRS figures, and individual facts govern the
details. Several source areas (infinite banking, offshore structures,
influencer business content) are advocacy-heavy; concept pages carry
their own caution sections.
