# Windfall Handling

A liquidity event — lottery win, IPO vest, inheritance, business sale,
legal settlement — skips you forward multiple tiers in a day. The
failure mode is speed: claims made before the team and structure
exist are permanent.

## Before Claiming / Announcement

1. **Sign and secure the instrument** — for a lottery ticket: sign
   it, photograph both sides, store it safe; until claimed it's a
   bearer document.
2. **Stay silent** — tell nobody beyond household. Every disclosure
   shrinks your options and expands the ask-list.
3. **Assemble the team first** — the required trio, engaged *before*
   claiming: fee-only financial advisor, tax attorney/CPA, estate
   attorney. This is the one situation where hiring fast at full
   price is correct.
4. **Decide entity/claim structure** — whether a trust or LLC can
   claim is **state-dependent**; some states allow anonymous claims
   via entity, most require a name. Your attorney answers this before
   anything is filed — claiming personally is irreversible.
5. **Lump-sum vs annuity** — for lottery/structured payouts: lump
   sum maximizes control and investment optionality (and dies with
   you if spent or mismanaged); annuity enforces discipline and keeps
   payments flowing. Model both after-tax with the tax advisor —
   there is no universal right answer, but *the decision is
   one-time and final*.

## The Order of Operations (first 90 days)

1. Tax reserve — park the estimated tax liability in T-bills/
   equivalent before any other use.
2. Estate core — will, revocable trust, POAs, beneficiaries — the
   [estate-planning-practices](https://github.com/levonk/skills-releases/blob/main/knowledge/estate-planning-practices/index.md)
   floor, now urgent instead of someday.
3. Insurance re-leveling — umbrella sized to the new net worth
   immediately (see
   [Insurance and Protection Levels](insurance-and-protection-levels.md)).
4. Giving plan — decide charitable intent *before* the ask-list
   finds you; a DAF absorbs a large same-year deduction efficiently
   (see
   [Charitable Giving Structures](charitable-giving-structures.md)).
5. Only then — investment deployment per the new tier's playbook
   (find it via [Wealth Tiers Roadmap](wealth-tiers-roadmap.md)).

## The Behavioral Layer

- **The ask-list is predictable** — family, friends, acquaintances,
  charities, scammers. Pre-decide the policy ("all requests go to the
  advisor/the foundation/no") before disclosure.
- **Do nothing structural for 6–12 months** — beyond the five
  urgent items above, large purchases, loans to friends, and business
  investments wait until the new-normal budget exists.
- **Treating the ticket/payout as a business asset** (assigning it to
  an entity to re-characterize the income) is flagged as IRS-scrutiny
  bait — entities for *claiming and privacy* are fine where legal;
  entities to *re-characterize winnings* are not.

## The Deduction Calendar

Windfall year is the highest-value year for: maxing all
tax-advantaged accounts, bunching charitable contributions into a
DAF, timing capital gains/losses, and — if the windfall is ongoing
(business income, not one-time) — the entity/residency evaluations
that become permanent moves. See
[Tax Optimization Ladder](tax-optimization-ladder.md).

## Cautions

- **Exemption and exclusion figures date** — annual gift exclusion
  and lifetime exemption amounts are legislated numbers; use
  current-year figures at execution time.
- **State tax on the windfall varies enormously** — the same jackpot
  nets differently in TX vs CA vs NY; if relocation is on the table,
  the domicile work (see
  [Residency and Domestic Havens](residency-and-domestic-havens.md))
  must genuinely precede the income event.
