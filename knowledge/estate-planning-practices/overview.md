# Estate Planning Practices — Overview

This bundle covers the **practical, legal, and disposition side** of
serious illness and death — the documents that must exist *before*
they're needed, because they are only writable while the person has
capacity.

## The Core Thesis

**Every adult needs four documents** — a will, a durable financial
power of attorney, a healthcare power of attorney, and an advance
directive (living will).[^kiplinger-docs] A serious diagnosis makes
"eventually" into "now": these documents are cheap and fast when the
signer has capacity, and impossible once they don't.

The second thesis: **the plan that can't be found doesn't exist.**
Documents, accounts, passwords, and wishes must be inventoried and the
inventory must be findable — see
[Documentation and Letter of Instruction](documentation-and-letter-of-instruction.md).

## The Document Map

```
While capable          If incapacitated              At death
─────────────          ────────────────              ────────
Will                   Durable financial POA ─┐      Will → executor → probate
Living trust           Healthcare POA ────────┤      Trust → successor trustee
Advance directive      Living will ───────────┤      Beneficiary designations
DNR / POLST            POLST orders ──────────┘      Disposition arrangements
Letter of instruction  (all take effect now)         After-death checklist
```

## Scope

- **Covers**: wills, revocable living trusts, durable powers of
  attorney (financial and healthcare), advance directives / living
  wills, DNR and POLST orders, organ donation, body disposition
  (cremation, burial, whole-body donation, commercial services such as
  Neptune Society), beneficiary designations, digital assets, the
  master documentation inventory, and the family's after-death
  checklist.
- **Does not cover**: tax optimization strategies, special-needs
  trusts, business succession, non-US jurisdictions — all attorney
  territory.
- **State law governs details.** Witnessing, notarization, and form
  requirements vary by state; free state-specific forms exist for
  directives, and an estate attorney is the escalation path for
  anything complex.

## How the Concepts Fit Together

1. **Decide who decides** — agents for finances and healthcare:
   [Powers of Attorney](powers-of-attorney.md).
2. **Record medical wishes** — resuscitation, ventilation, feeding,
   comfort care:
   [Advance Directives and Resuscitation](advance-directives-and-resuscitation.md).
3. **Direct the assets** — will and/or living trust:
   [Core Estate Documents](core-estate-documents.md);
   beneficiary designations and accounts:
   [Digital Estate and Beneficiaries](digital-estate-and-beneficiaries.md).
4. **Arrange disposition** — cremation/burial/donation, pre-need vs
   at-need: [Body Disposition](body-disposition.md).
5. **Make it all findable** —
   [Documentation and Letter of Instruction](documentation-and-letter-of-instruction.md).
6. **Leave the family a checklist** —
   [After-Death Checklist](after-death-checklist.md).
