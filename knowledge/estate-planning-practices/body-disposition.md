# Body Disposition

## The Main Options

- **Direct cremation** — the body is cremated shortly after death
  without embalming, viewing, or ceremony. The simplest and cheapest
  mainstream option; memorial services happen separately, on the
  family's schedule.
- **Traditional burial** — funeral home + casket + cemetery plot;
  typically the most expensive path ($7,000–$15,000+ once plot, vault,
  and marker are included).
- **Whole-body donation** — medical schools and research programs
  accept bodies at little or no cost; cremated remains are usually
  returned to the family after study (1–2 years). Register in advance
  — donation can't be arranged at the last minute.
- **Green/natural burial** — no embalming, biodegradable container,
  conservation cemetery; availability varies widely by region.
- **Cremation with ceremony** — viewing/service before cremation adds
  embalming and facility fees to the direct-cremation base.

## Direct Cremation: Neptune Society and Alternatives

**Neptune Society** is the largest US direct-cremation provider —
a commercial, one-call service: they transport the body from the place
of death to a climate-controlled facility, handle permits, file the
death certificate, cremate, and return or scatter the remains (at sea,
or to next of kin, or to a cemetery — urn included).[^neptune-gpl]

Real published pricing (Jan 2026 general price lists):[^neptune-gpl][^neptune-central-ca]

| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Direct cremation package (San Diego) | ~$1,992 |
| Direct cremation package (San Jose area) | ~$2,395 |
| Travel protection add-on | ~+$395 |
| Certified death certificate copies | ~$26 each (county fee) |
| Disposition permit | ~$12 |

Their packages include the logistics families otherwise scramble for:
transportation, licensed holding, permit procurement, death-certificate
filing, Social Security and VA benefit filing assistance, and remains
scattering or return.[^neptune-central-ca]

**Cheaper alternatives:** local independent crematories and funeral
homes that offer direct cremation often price well below national
chains — typically $800–$2,000 depending on metro area. Compare at
least three providers' General Price Lists; the
[FTC Funeral Rule](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/ftc-funeral-rule)
entitles you to itemized prices by phone or in writing without giving
personal information. Costco members and some religious/community
organizations also have negotiated cremation pricing.

## Pre-Need vs At-Need

- **At-need** — arranged after death; the family calls, signs, pays.
- **Pre-need** — arranged and paid in advance; locks the plan and
  removes the burden from grieving family. Pre-need payments go into a
  state-required trust fund per state law.[^neptune-faqs]

For someone with a serious diagnosis, pre-need is the gift: one phone
call executes the whole plan and the family never negotiates a funeral
home under grief.

## Practical Points

- **The body can't wait.** At death, someone must call the disposition
  provider (or hospice, which coordinates). Put the chosen provider's
  number in the
  [letter of instruction](documentation-and-letter-of-instruction.md)
  and the [after-death checklist](after-death-checklist.md).
- **Order enough death certificates** — estates routinely need 10–20
  certified copies for banks, insurers, titles; they're cheap when
  ordered with the disposition and slower to get later.
- **Veterans** — VA burial benefits (plot, marker, some transport) may
  apply; Neptune-type providers will help file, or contact the VA
  directly.
- **Write the wishes down.** Burial vs cremation vs donation, service
  preferences, and provider choice all belong in the will's wishes
  section and the letter of instruction.

## Common Mistakes

- **Comparing packages, not price lists.** Ask for the General Price
  List and compare *direct cremation* line items — packages bundle
  varying extras (urns, scattering, travel coverage).
- **Assuming "cremation" quotes are apples-to-apples.** Some quotes
  exclude transport radius, container, permits, or the crematory fee
  itself.
- **Leaving it undecided.** An undocumented preference becomes a
  family argument at the worst moment.
