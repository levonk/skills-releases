# Powers of Attorney

A power of attorney (POA) designates an **agent** to act for you (the
**principal**) when you can't act yourself. Two separate documents are
needed because they cover different domains.[^nia-affairs]

## Durable Financial POA

Authorizes the agent to manage finances: pay bills, manage accounts,
file taxes, handle insurance, sell property if needed. "Durable" means
the authority **continues if you lose capacity** — without the durable
clause, the POA evaporates exactly when it's most
needed.[^kiplinger-docs]

- Some POAs take effect on signing; others are "springing" (activate
  on incapacity). Springing POAs can create proof-of-incapacity
  friction precisely when speed matters — know which kind you're
  signing.
- **The POA dies with you.** At death, the will/trust and
  executor/trustee take over — the agent's authority ends.

## Healthcare POA (Healthcare Proxy)

Names the person who makes **medical** decisions when you can't
communicate — covering situations your
[advance directive](advance-directives-and-resuscitation.md) doesn't
anticipate.[^nia-affairs] The proxy should:

- Know your values and wishes — actually discuss them, don't just
  name them
- Live close enough to reach the hospital, or be able to travel
- Be able to disagree with doctors under pressure and with family
  members who want a different call

Name a primary **and an alternate** for both POAs.

## The Capacity Deadline

Both documents require the principal to have **legal capacity at
signing**. After significant cognitive decline, the documents can't be
created — families then face guardianship/conservatorship proceedings,
which are slow, public, and expensive. This is the strongest argument
for doing these early in a serious diagnosis, not "when needed."

## Common Mistakes

- **Only doing one.** Financial POA doesn't authorize medical
  decisions; healthcare POA can't pay the mortgage. Both.
- **Choosing by affection rather than competence.** The right agent is
  organized, nearby, and steady under pressure.
- **Not telling the agents.** An agent who discovers their role during
  a crisis is starting at zero — walk them through the documents and
  where they live (see
  [Documentation and Letter of Instruction](documentation-and-letter-of-instruction.md)).
- **Assuming spouses have automatic authority.** Marriage does not
  grant financial or medical decision authority in most contexts —
  the documents are still required.
