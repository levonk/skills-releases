# Cancer Patient Practices

A compounding knowledge base for navigating a new cancer diagnosis — from
the first days after diagnosis through treatment decisions, clinical
trials, financial navigation, and (when relevant) palliative and hospice
care. Each concept captures a specific practice that helps a patient or
caregiver get the right care faster, avoid common failure modes, and make
decisions that match the patient's values.

The practices are synthesized from a first-person account of a 2026
squamous cell carcinoma diagnosis, National Cancer Institute (NCI) and
American Cancer Society (ACS) patient guidance, NCCN patient resources,
and hard-won community experience. They are general — applicable to most
newly diagnosed patients — not medical advice for any specific case.
