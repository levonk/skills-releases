# Auction Sourcing Practices

A compounding knowledge base documenting practices for sourcing through
auction channels — government surplus, seized property, salvage, tax deed,
and corporate liquidation auctions. Each concept captures a specific failure
mode or constraint that is unique to auctions and the practice that prevents
it, sourced from real auction platform research and the
`shopping-deal-intelligence` skill's auction references.

## Concepts

* [Overview](overview.md.tmpl) - Synthesis of the auction sourcing practice set and how the pieces fit together
* [Aggregator vs Direct Source](aggregator-vs-direct-source.md) - Aggregators are tracked but not actively searched; use the underlying platforms directly; keep both when a government site redirects to an auctioneer
* [Vehicle Smog and CARB Compliance](vehicle-smog-carb-compliance.md) - Auction vehicles may not be certifiable for registration in strict-emissions states; check the emissions label before bidding
* [Export-Only Vehicle Restrictions](export-only-vehicle-restrictions.md) - Some auction vehicles cannot be titled in the US; the buyer must export them within 30-90 days
* [Salvage and Rebuilt Title Brands](salvage-rebuilt-title-brands.md) - Auction vehicles frequently have branded titles with significant implications for financing, insurance, and resale
* [Damage Level Opt-In](damage-level-opt-in.md) - Auction vehicles are sold with varying levels of disclosed damage; the user must explicitly opt into each level
* [Dealer License Gating](dealer-license-gating.md) - Some auction sales require a dealer license; public buyers can use a dealer agent/broker or stick to public-access sales
* [Property Buildability Checklist](property-buildability-checklist.md) - Before bidding on raw land at auction, verify ingress, topography, zoning, lot dimensions, easements, utilities, and road access
* [Auction Title Risks](auction-title-risks.md) - Many auction property sales do not provide title insurance; surviving liens, occupants, and redemption rights are risks
* [Total Acquisition Cost](total-acquisition-cost.md) - The winning bid is rarely the final price; always calculate bid + buyer's premium + tax + fees + transport + repairs
* [Locale and Travel Filtering](locale-and-travel-filtering.md) - Auction locale determines whether a user can participate; filter by travel radius and pickup requirements
