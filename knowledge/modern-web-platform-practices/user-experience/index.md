# User Experience

* [Maximize content and reduce noise](content-first-noise-reduction.md) - Users come for the content. Keep the page frame small, move it out of the way while users read, and show secondary content only when asked.

* [Adapt to the form factor](form-factor-adaptation.md) - The same interface runs on phones, laptops, and foldables, with touch, mouse, or keyboard. Size controls for the input, let components adapt to their container, and keep text and controls clear of system UI.

* [Implement natural interactions](natural-interactions.md) - Interfaces feel natural when every input gets an immediate response, motion behaves like a physical object, content follows the user's finger, and nothing depends on a mouse.
