# Tax Optimization Ladder

Tax optimization is ordered — each rung builds on the ones below it.
Doing rung 6 while rung 2 is undone is the classic HNW mistake: a
foreign entity while the HSA sits empty.

## The Ladder

1. **Tax-advantaged accounts (Tiers 1–2)** — 401(k)/IRA/HSA/529
   maxed in the right order (see
   [Foundation and Accumulation](foundation-and-accumulation.md)).
   The HSA's triple advantage makes it the best account in the code.
2. **Asset location (~Tier 3)** — tax-inefficient assets (bonds,
   REITs, high-turnover funds) inside tax-advantaged accounts;
   tax-efficient index ETFs in taxable. Free alpha — same portfolio,
   less tax.
3. **Tax-loss harvesting (~Tier 3)** — systematic harvesting in
   taxable accounts; mind wash-sale rules.
4. **Charitable structures (~Tier 4–5)** — appreciated-asset
   donations (deduct FMV, never pay the gain), DAFs for bunching and
   pre-funding; CLTs and foundations later. See
   [Charitable Giving Structures](charitable-giving-structures.md).
5. **Strategic residency (~Tier 4–5)** — domicile in a
   no-income-tax state (FL, TX, NV, WY, SD, AK, WA) when your facts
   support it; the domicile-proof checklist matters more than the
   mailing address. Abroad: FEIE excludes ~$126k+ of foreign earned
   income (indexed) for qualifying expats.[^irs-feie] See
   [Residency and Domestic Havens](residency-and-domestic-havens.md).
6. **Corporate domicile (~Tier 5+)** — situs holding and operating
   entities in favorable states (DE/NV/WY); see
   [Entity Structuring and Domicile](entity-structuring-and-domicile.md).
7. **Domestic trust havens (~Tier 6–7)** — situs irrevocable trusts
   in SD/NV/WY/AK for state-income-tax-free accumulation and dynasty
   duration; see
   [Residency and Domestic Havens](residency-and-domestic-havens.md).
8. **International structures (~Tier 7–8)** — foreign entities and
   trusts with full US compliance overhead (CFC/NCTI, §962, FBAR,
   FATCA). For US persons these are almost never tax *avoidance* —
   they're market access, asset protection, or genuine relocation.
   See [International Structures](international-structures.md).
9. **Buy-borrow-die (throughout the top)** — at Tier 5+, borrowing
   against appreciated assets instead of selling defers/eliminates
   the capital gain and preserves the step-up at death. See
   [Borrowing Against Assets](borrowing-against-assets.md).

## Billionaire-Style Moves, Honest Framing

The publicized "loopholes" — buy-borrow-die, carried interest, PPLI,
step-up basis, GRATs, FLPs — are mostly **deferral plus step-up**
mechanics, not magic: appreciated assets are borrowed against rather
than sold, and the basis resets at death. They are legal, they are
available at Tier 5+, and each has a cost floor (legal fees, interest,
compliance) that makes it pointless at lower tiers.

## Cautions

- **Compliance beats cleverness.** The IRS penalty regime for missed
  international filings (FBAR, 8938, 5471) starts at five figures
  *per form per year* — worse than most tax bills these strategies
  save.
- **State audits.** High-tax states audit claimed domicile changes;
  the residency checklist exists because "I bought a condo in
  Florida" does not move your domicile.
- **Figures drift.** Contribution limits, QBI thresholds, FEIE
  amounts, and the estate exemption change annually — verify the
  current year before acting on any number here.

[^irs-feie]: IRS, "Foreign Earned Income Exclusion" — §911 exclusion
    amount is indexed annually.
