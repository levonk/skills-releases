# Wealth Planning Practices — Overview

This bundle covers **what to do next with your money at every stage** —
organized by net-worth tier, because the correct next action changes
with scale. A household at $80k needs a different playbook than one at
$3M, and the $3M household's playbook is wrong at $30M.

## The Core Thesis

**Net worth is the primary axis; income is the secondary axis.**
[Wealth Tiers Roadmap](wealth-tiers-roadmap.md) defines eight tiers
from Foundation (<$100k) to UHNW ($50M+). Each tier specifies its own
investment posture, professional team, legal documents, and insurance
levels. High income with low net worth still starts at Tier 1 for
foundation documents and insurance — income only accelerates how fast
you climb.

The second thesis: **the $1M–$30M band is a financial-services
desert.**[^nelson-mfo] Too wealthy for cookie-cutter advisors, below
the ~$100M single-family-office threshold. The Micro Family Office
model — a fractional team of tax strategist, estate attorney,
bookkeeper, and advisors run like a business — is the response (see
[Micro Family Office](micro-family-office.md)).

The third thesis: **structures compound, and order matters.** Three
ladders run through the bundle — protection (insurance → entities →
titling → DAPT → offshore), tax (accounts → location → harvesting →
charitable → residency → domicile → havens → international), and
liquidity (cash → HELOC → policy loans → SBLOC/margin →
buy-borrow-die). Each rung has a tier where it turns on; grabbing a
rung early is wasted complexity, late is leaked money.

## Scope

- **Covers**: net-worth-tiered planning, tax-advantaged accumulation,
  advisor selection, insurance levels, entity structuring (LLC,
  S-Corp, holding companies), asset protection, tax optimization,
  residency and trust situs, advanced estate instruments, charitable
  structures, infinite/family banking, borrowing against assets,
  micro family office operations, family governance, international
  structures, windfalls, and business acquisition.
- **Does not cover**: investment selection (this bundle is
  asset-class-agnostic — it covers structures and sequencing, not
  stock picks), tax preparation, consumer debt counseling, or non-US
  base-layer planning (international content covers US persons holding
  foreign entities, not emigrating).
- **US-centric.** Tax code references (QBI, §962, FBAR/FATCA, estate
  exemption), entity law (LLCs, DAPTs), and professional categories
  (CPA, CFP, fee-only) are US. Dollar thresholds and figures are
  dated — verify current-year numbers before acting.

## Source Posture

A substantial share of the source corpus is **advocacy material** —
infinite-banking practitioners sell policies, offshore promoters sell
structures, influencer content sells attention. Each concept page
carries a caution section; the pattern-level rule is: *advocacy
material teaches the mechanics well and the drawbacks poorly.* Read
[Infinite Banking and Family Banking](infinite-banking-and-family-bank.md)
and [International Structures](international-structures.md) for the
most contested areas.

## Relationship to Estate Planning

This bundle covers *wealth-side* instruments (GRATs, ILITs, dynasty
trusts). The *document-side* floor — will, living trust, powers of
attorney, advance directives, disposition — lives in
[estate-planning-practices](../estate-planning-practices/index.md),
which applies from Tier 1. The `wealth-planning` skill walks both.
