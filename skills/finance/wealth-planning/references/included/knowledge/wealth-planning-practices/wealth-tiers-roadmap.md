# Wealth Tiers Roadmap

A threshold-based roadmap: each tier defines, at a net-worth and
income band, **how much to invest and where**, **which professionals
to engage**, **which documents to have**, and **what insurance to
hold**. The point is knowing what changes the moment you cross a band.

## The Axis Rule

Thresholds are keyed on **net worth** (primary) cross-referenced with
**income** (cash-flow capacity — it determines what professionals and
insurance you can actually afford). A high earner with low net worth
still starts at Tier 1 for foundation documents and insurance, then
jumps forward on investment allocation as cash flow allows.

## The Master Matrix

| Tier | Name | Net Worth | Typical Income | Primary Focus |
|------|------|-----------|----------------|---------------|
| 1 | Foundation | <$100k | <$100k | Stability, habits, baseline docs |
| 2 | Stability | $100k–$500k | $100k–$250k | Debt payoff, max tax-advantaged accounts |
| 3 | Mass Affluent | $500k–$1M | $250k–$500k | Diversified accumulation, first entity |
| 4 | HNW Entry | $1M–$5M | $500k–$1M | Tax planning, revocable trust, umbrella |
| 5 | Established HNW | $5M–$10M | $1M+ | Asset protection, irrevocable trusts, IBC |
| 6 | VHNW (MFO Zone) | $10M–$30M | portfolio-driven | Micro family office, advanced estate |
| 7 | Upper VHNW | $30M–$50M | portfolio-driven | GRATs, CLATs, DAPTs, jurisdictional planning |
| 8 | UHNW | $50M+ | portfolio-driven | Family office, dynasty trusts, private foundation |

Tier 3 is the last tier where "just buy index funds" is a complete
strategy. Tiers 4–6 are the **financial-services desert** — see
[Micro Family Office](micro-family-office.md).[^nelson-mfo]

## Documents by Tier (summary)

| Document | First required | Revisit |
|----------|---------------|---------|
| Beneficiary designations | Tier 1 | Every tier, annually |
| Simple will, financial POA, healthcare directive | Tier 1 | Tier 4 |
| Revocable living trust + pour-over will | Tier 4 | Tier 5 (fund with entities) |
| LLCs (business / rentals) | Tier 3–4 | Tier 5 (holding-co layering) |
| ILIT | Tier 5 | Tier 6+ |
| DAPT | Tier 6 | Tier 7 |
| GRAT / CLAT / FLP, dynasty trust, governance docs | Tier 7 | Tier 8 |
| Private foundation / PTC, offshore trust | Tier 8 | — |

Core documents (will, POA, trust funding, directives) are detailed in
the estate bundle — see
[estate-planning-practices](../estate-planning-practices/index.md).

## Graduation Triggers

- **Liquidity events** (business sale, IPO, inheritance, lottery)
  skip you forward multiple tiers — run
  [Windfall Handling](windfall-handling.md) first, then re-plan at the
  new tier.
- **Income before net worth** — a $500k earner at $300k net worth
  buys Tier 1–2 insurance/documents immediately but can run Tier 3–4
  accumulation moves in parallel.
- **Complexity before net worth** — a business with $300k profit or a
  rental portfolio pulls entity structuring forward regardless of
  total net worth (see
  [Entity Structuring and Domicile](entity-structuring-and-domicile.md)).

## How to Use

1. Find your tier by net worth; sanity-check against income.
2. Work the tier's playbook — and read **one tier ahead** to
   pre-position (e.g., start the IBC conversation at Tier 4 though it
   matures at Tier 5).
3. Re-read your tier annually and after any liquidity event.
