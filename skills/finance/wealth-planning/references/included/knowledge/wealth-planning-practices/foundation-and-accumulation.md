# Foundation and Accumulation (Tiers 1–3)

The accumulation engine for the first ~$1M. Everything here is boring
on purpose — at these tiers, savings rate and sequencing beat
sophistication.

## The Ordering (Tiers 1–2)

1. **Capture the employer match** — the 401(k) match is an instant
   ~100% return; nothing else comes first.
2. **Starter emergency fund** — a small buffer (~$1k–1 month) before
   aggressive debt paydown, so the next surprise doesn't go back on a
   card.
3. **Kill high-interest debt** — credit cards and personal loans
   (~>8–10% APR) are a guaranteed negative return; pay them off before
   taxable investing.
4. **Full emergency fund** — 3–6 months of expenses in a
   high-yield savings account.
5. **Max tax-advantaged accounts** — the sequence: 401(k) to match →
   HSA (triple tax advantage: deductible in, tax-free growth, tax-free
   out for medical) → IRA/Roth IRA → 401(k) to the annual max →
   taxable brokerage.
6. **Backdoor Roth** — once income exceeds Roth IRA limits, the
   non-deductible-IRA → Roth-conversion path keeps the Roth benefit.
   Watch the pro-rata rule if you hold pre-tax IRA balances.
7. **Baseline documents and insurance** — beneficiary designations on
   every account, simple will, financial POA, healthcare directive
   (all Tier 1 per the [roadmap](wealth-tiers-roadmap.md)); term life
   if anyone depends on your income; disability insurance for W-2
   earners; $1M umbrella by Tier 2–3.

## Tier 3 Additions ($500k–$1M, income ~$250k–$500k)

- **Invest 30–40% of gross income.** Savings rate still dominates;
  this is the last tier where index-fund-only is a complete strategy.
- **Diversify beyond index funds** — real estate at ~10–25% of the
  portfolio becomes reasonable (see
  [Business Acquisition and Income](business-acquisition-and-income.md)
  for the honest counterweights).
- **Asset location + systematic tax-loss harvesting** — hold
  bonds/REITs in tax-advantaged accounts, tax-efficient index ETFs in
  taxable; harvest losses on a schedule, not on vibes (see
  [Tax Optimization Ladder](tax-optimization-ladder.md)).
- **Formal estate plan** — attorney-drafted will package now; the
  revocable living trust lands at Tier 4.
- **First entity evaluation** — self-employment profit above
  roughly $80–100k is the S-Corp math zone; business/rental activity
  pulls LLCs forward (see
  [Entity Structuring and Domicile](entity-structuring-and-domicile.md)).
- **Residency math** — high earners in CA/NY start pricing the
  no-income-tax-state domicile move (see
  [Residency and Domestic Havens](residency-and-domestic-havens.md)).

## What to Avoid at These Tiers

- **AUM advisors charging ~1%** on small balances — the fee is a
  meaningful drag and there are no assets worth managing yet.
- **Commissioned insurance products** (whole life, IUL) sold as
  "investments" — IBC-type policies are a Tier 4–5 conversation at
  the earliest (see
  [Infinite Banking and Family Banking](infinite-banking-and-family-bank.md)).
- **Complexity without assets** — trusts beyond a simple will,
  multi-entity stacks, and offshore anything are premature below
  Tier 3.

## The Bridge to Tier 4

You graduate when net worth crosses ~$1M or income makes taxes the
dominant leak. The Tier 4 shift: written investment thesis, tax
strategist (not just a filing CPA), funded revocable trust, umbrella
scaled to net worth, and the first borrowing-against-assets facility.
