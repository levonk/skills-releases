# FCEV Vehicle Catalog — Reference

Fuel cell vehicles sold in the US market. All were sold only in California
due to hydrogen infrastructure limitations. This limits salvage availability
to California yards.

Referenced by `INSTRUCTIONS.md` section "Fuel Cell Vehicles in the US Market".

## Vehicles NOT Sold in the US Retail Market

These FCEVs exist globally but were not sold retail in the US — they will
not appear in US salvage yards. Listed here so the AI does not waste search
budget on them:

- Mercedes-Benz B-Class F-CELL — lease-only, Europe
- Honda FCX Clarity (2008–2014) — lease-only, predecessor to Clarity Fuel Cell
- Toyota FCHV-adv — research/lease vehicle only
- BMW iX5 Hydrogen — prototype/fleet only
