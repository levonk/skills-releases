# Career Advancement Practices

A compounding knowledge base documenting evidence-backed practices for the
candidate side of the hiring pipeline — from resume writing through salary
negotiation. Each concept captures a specific discipline that makes a
candidate more likely to pass ATS parsing, survive the 7-second recruiter
scan, convert into an interview, navigate the interview process, and
negotiate a strong offer.

The concepts are synthesized from recruiter interviews, eye-tracking
studies, and analyses of 125,000+ resumes. They are general — applicable
to any job seeker — not specific to any individual candidate.
