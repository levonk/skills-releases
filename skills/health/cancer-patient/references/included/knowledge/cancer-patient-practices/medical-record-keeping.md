# Medical Record Keeping

## The Single Folder System

One folder — physical, digital, or mirrored both ways — holds
everything about the case. In the seed case, a dedicated Mac folder
received dictated notes after every appointment, which AI then turned
into dated summaries with follow-up questions for next
time.[^maxwell-cancer-2026] The payoff: a searchable history to refer
to across months and across providers who don't share record systems.

## What Goes In

- **Pathology reports** — and the slides/blocks themselves are
  requestable for second-opinion re-reads (see
  [Second Opinions and Cancer Centers](second-opinions-and-cancer-centers.md))
- **Imaging** — reports plus the image files (disc or portal export)
- **Lab and biomarker/genetic results** — see
  [Genetic and Biomarker Testing](genetic-and-biomarker-testing.md)
- **Visit summaries** — dictated after each appointment, before memory
  decays
- **The follow-up list** — promised orders, referrals, callbacks (see
  [Scheduling Persistence](scheduling-persistence.md))
- **Medication list** — current prescriptions, supplements, allergies
- **Insurance correspondence** — approvals, denials, appeal letters
  (see
  [Insurance and Financial Toxicity](insurance-and-financial-toxicity.md))
- **Care team directory** — names, roles, phone numbers, portal
  addresses

## The Appointment Loop

1. **Before**: review open questions; bring the written list (doctors
   answer what you ask — many wait for you to ask).
2. **During**: take notes or record (ask permission; most clinicians
   allow it), or have the caregiver take notes.
3. **After**: dictate what happened and what was said; produce the
   dated summary and next-visit question list; file any new reports.

## Common Mistakes

- **Keeping records in portal silos.** Each health system's portal
  shows only its own slice; the folder is the unified record.
- **Requesting the report but not the source.** Second opinions need
  slides and image files, not summaries.
- **Trusting memory for verbal answers.** Dictate within the hour —
  details decay fast, and the details are what the next provider asks
  for.
