# Patient Advocacy and Support

## Organizations Worth a Call

- **Sijbrandij Foundation — Free Cancer Care Team.** Founded by Sid
  Sijbrandij (GitLab cofounder) after he took his own case management
  into his own hands when standard of care ran out. They maintain
  practical materials and question lists, and take calls — the seed
  author recommends scheduling one.[^sijbrandij-foundation] Especially
  relevant when evaluating options beyond or outside standard care.
- **American Cancer Society (ACS)** — 24/7 helpline, treatment
  decision guides, lodging and transport programs, and the ACS ACTS
  clinical-trials matching service.[^acs-after-diagnosis]
- **NCI Cancer Information Service** — 1-800-4-CANCER for tailored
  trial searches and answers (see [Clinical Trials](clinical-trials.md)).
- **CancerCare** — oncology social workers, counseling, case
  management, and limited financial assistance.
- **Disease-specific foundations** — nearly every cancer type has one
  (blood cancers, breast, lung, colorectal, head and neck…). They run
  the patient-to-patient connections recommended in
  [Treatment Decision Making](treatment-decision-making.md), plus
  co-pay funds and helplines.
- **Patient navigator / oncology social worker** — the in-system
  advocate; ask for one at the first visit. They know the hospital's
  shortcuts: charity care, records transfers, scheduling workarounds.

## Structured Caregiver Roles

The practices in this bundle produce a natural division of labor —
assign explicitly rather than hoping coverage happens:

| Role | Owns |
|------|------|
| Caller | [Scheduling Persistence](scheduling-persistence.md) — appointments, cancellation lists, follow-ups |
| Recorder | [Medical Record Keeping](medical-record-keeping.md) — folder, dictated summaries, question lists |
| Researcher | [Standard of Care Navigation](standard-of-care-navigation.md), [Clinical Trials](clinical-trials.md), second-opinion logistics |
| Money | [Insurance and Financial Toxicity](insurance-and-financial-toxicity.md) — insurer contact, bills, assistance applications |
| Presence | Accompanies to appointments — second set of ears is the cheapest accuracy upgrade |

## Common Mistakes

- **Doing it alone.** These organizations exist because this is hard;
  a 20-minute call to a foundation or navigator routinely surfaces
  options the patient didn't know existed.
- **Keeping caregivers as bystanders.** Give them named roles — most
  people want to help and don't know how.
- **Only seeking support late.** Peer connection and navigator help
  compound; starting early beats starting in a crisis.
