# Estate Planning Practices

A compounding knowledge base for the non-medical side of serious
illness and end of life: the legal documents that direct assets and
decision-making, advance directives that record resuscitation and
life-sustaining-treatment wishes, body disposition arrangements
(cremation, burial, donation), and the documentation that lets a
family act without a scavenger hunt.

The practices are synthesized from National Institute on Aging,
American Cancer Society, Kiplinger, FreeWill, and consumer-protection
(FTC Funeral Rule) guidance, plus commercial-disposition pricing
sources. They are general US guidance — state law governs the details,
and an estate-planning attorney is the right escalation for anything
beyond simple estates.
