# Universal Banned Patterns (Slop Lint)

Transferable across all clients. Client-specific entries get added per
engagement via the feedback ledger (Phase 5). Patterns appearing twice get
promoted here or to the client's persona constraints.

## Core style rules

- No dashes (em-dash, en-dash) — use commas or periods.
- No staccato chains (three or more one-line paragraphs in a row).
- Active voice.
- Banned words: shift, gap, leap, gains, solid, practical, clicked, shifted,
  "the result was clear:".
- No vague statements.
- No "it's not x, it's y" or any antithesis / negation-mirror / then-now
  template.
- No question segues ("So what's the answer? ...").
- No "The focus is simple."-family phrases.
- No "No speeches. No discussion." format.
- Plain operational language.
- Nothing with "actually matter".

## Learned universals

- No dramatized pre-framing (state the fact; the number does the flinching).
- No poetic metaphor; mechanical/institutional analogies only.
- No wistful/confessional closers.
- No symmetric antithesis machines.
- Punchline stacking = strongest AI signature (budget measured per client).
- No glue sentences (every sentence carries a fact).
- Quarry narrative posts from real transcript sentences, never generate
  connective tissue.
- No press-release verbs (earmarked, unveiled, slated).
- No abstract back-referents ("results like that").
- Introduce every named artifact; translate mechanisms to plain words.
- No positioning tropes ("sits at the intersection of").
- No engineered thesis sentences (short claim, then everyday mechanism).
- Abstraction subjects must be able to do their verbs (money can't hire,
  checking can't be people).
- No sweeping absolutes ("nobody is talking about").
- No folksy idioms outside the client's measured casual register.
- Reader-biography claims must hold for the whole audience.
- Dates/temporal markers only from sources.
- No withheld-punchline structure (setup → beat → bare-number reveal); state
  the claim in sentence one and explain.
- Category words hide the thing; concrete instances first — but verify
  "concrete" against the client's register (fragments for rhythm failed for
  one client).
- No "us included" style mid-sentence self-inclusion parentheticals.
- Conventional ≠ crafted: the client's ordinary pleasantries and repetitions
  are allowed; crafted language is the tell.

## How to use this list

1. Seed the client's `banned-patterns.md` with this entire list on first
   setup.
2. During Phase 4 Gate B (banned patterns check), scan every draft against
   this list plus the client-specific entries.
3. During Phase 5 (feedback loop), when a pattern appears twice in client
   corrections, promote it to the client's `banned-patterns.md`.
4. When a client-specific pattern is genuinely universal (would apply to any
   founder), propose promoting it here in the knowledge bundle's
   `founder-content-system.md` concept page.
